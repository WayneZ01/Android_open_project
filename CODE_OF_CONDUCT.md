# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and technically constructive community. Contributions and reviews should focus on verifiable behavior, project quality, and the needs of Android developers.

Examples of positive behavior include giving actionable feedback, acknowledging mistakes, respecting different experience levels, and disclosing relevant project affiliations.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, deliberate misinformation, spam, and publication of private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the lead maintainer through the repository security-contact path described in `SECURITY.md`. The maintainer may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented when it is safe to do so, and avoid exposing reporters or sensitive information.
