# Maintainers

## Lead maintainer

- [WayneZ01](https://github.com/WayneZ01) — project owner and lead maintainer

The lead maintainer has final responsibility for catalog scope, pull-request review, issue triage, releases, automation, and security response.

## Becoming a maintainer

Regular contributors may be invited to help maintain the project after demonstrating sustained, constructive participation. Useful contributions include validating links, improving descriptions, reviewing categories, resolving issues, and helping contributors.

Maintainer access is granted gradually and follows least privilege. Repository write access is never required for ordinary contributions.

## Inactive maintainers

Maintainers who are unavailable for an extended period may step back voluntarily. Access may be adjusted when necessary to protect the project. Prior contributions remain credited in Git history.
